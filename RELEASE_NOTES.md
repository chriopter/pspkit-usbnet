Bring your PSP online over USB — including the PSP Street.

- **Street support:** original Network Settings menu and icon restored, no flashing.
- **Standalone gateways:** Windows, Linux x86_64 / ARM64, macOS Intel / Apple Silicon.
- **Improved CLI:** clearer connection status, startup errors and reconnect handling.
- **PSPLink support:** networking and debugging on the same cable.

Download `usbnet.prx` and the gateway for your computer below. Setup, demo and
verification details are in the [README](https://github.com/chriopter/pspkit-usbnet#readme).
